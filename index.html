About.

I'm a PhD candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Iowa State University. My work sits at the intersection of analog circuit design, chemelectric sensors, wireless power and data transfer, and power management.

In 2019, I joined Dr. Chenchang Zhan's Energy Efficient Integrated Circuit Lab (EEIC) at the School of Microelectronics, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I worked on power management IC design.

Since January 2023, I've been at Iowa State advised by Dr. Cheng Huang, focusing on chemelectric sensor circuits for implantable systems.

In summer 2026, I completed an internship at Skyworks Solutions, Inc. as an Analog/Power IC Design Intern.
